Cut the Cable Cord

Tired of paying $200+ a month for cable? I'll sit down with you in your home, look at your current cable and internet bill, and show you exactly how much you can save by switching to streaming. Most of my clients cut their monthly TV and internet bill in half — often more.

Internet — pick one

  • Xfinity 500 Mbps — 5-year price-lock plan. Reliable, widely available in The Villages. Great for households that want one bill for internet.
  • Quantum Fiber 500 Mbps for Life — lifetime price guarantee with no contract. Symmetrical fiber speeds. Best long-term value where available.

Live TV — pick one

  • YouTube TV ($89/mo) — best if you want NFL Sunday Ticket (out-of-market pro football, add-on ~$300/season). Unlimited DVR, clean interface.
  • Hulu + Live TV with Disney+ and ESPN+ ($83/mo) — best value if you don't need Sunday Ticket. Includes Disney+ and ESPN+ in the price, so it covers movies, kids' content, and live sports in one package.

Movies & on-demand

  • Netflix — for movies and original series. I'll help you pick the right plan and set up profiles for everyone in the house.

How I decide which TV package is right for you

I ask one simple question: "Do you see yourself ever paying around $300 for NFL Sunday Ticket to watch out-of-market pro football games this season?"

  • Yes → YouTube TV, because Sunday Ticket is only available through YouTube.
  • No → Hulu + Live TV, because it includes Disney+ and ESPN+ for a lower total price — the better value.
