Cut the Cable Cord

This is one of our most popular services, but it's just one piece of what we do. If you're tired of paying $200+ a month for cable, we'll walk you through a clear, two-step process.

1

The Consultation

  • Bill audit. We review your current cable, internet, and streaming bills line by line.
  • TV compatibility check. We examine each TV in your home to confirm it can support modern streaming, and identify anything that technically blocks the capability to undertake this goal.
  • Remedy suggestions. If a TV or device needs an upgrade, we tell you exactly what's needed — no pressure, no upsell.
  • Jargon demystified. We give you a clear overview of the terms associated with modern streaming so you understand the landscape: Smart Technology, Internet Provider (IP), Streaming, Platform, IPTV, and more.
  • Recommendation. Based on a few questions about your viewing habits, we tell you your best options — which in most cases undercut what you're currently paying by a large margin while providing a service that is superior to what you have now.
2

The Install

  • We return on a separate day to set up everything decided during the consultation.
  • Every account is created, every app is installed, every device is connected and signed in.
  • We arrange the home screen, label everything clearly, and walk you through how to use it before we leave.
  • Streaming subscriptions and internet service are billed directly to you by those companies — our service fee is separate.

Two appointments, two visits

The consultation and the install are two separate appointments. Each appointment is compensated on the day of that visit. The amount is up to you — see Pricing below for how that works.

  1. You call and leave a voicemail. Mike's phone is on Do Not Disturb during the day — all calls go to voicemail, and that's the only way to set an appointment. Erin can be reached by text or voicemail.

  4. We explain as we go. No jargon, no rushing. You see every step and every password. Nothing is hidden.
  5. You pay what you feel it's worth. That's the pricing model for most services. See below for details.
  6. We're a phone call away after. If something stops working the next day, we'll talk you through it or come back — no charge for a quick follow-up.

Pricing — Pay What You Feel It's Worth

For most services, we don't charge an hourly rate and we don't quote a price up front. After the work is done, you pay whatever amount reflects the value you feel we brought to your life.

We've worked this way for years and our clients have been generous. It works because we take the time to do the job right, and because there's no pressure and no awkward negotiation. If you feel we didn't help, you don't pay. Simple as that.

Exceptions: Three services have fixed prices because they include hardware or software we provide — the Golf Cart Theft Tracker ($100), Villages Gate RF Tag ($50–$100), and Microsoft Office 2021 Lifetime License ($75).

Why we don't charge by the hour

What you see as an insurmountable technical issue is often something we've fixed five times that week alone. We're in and out in most cases in minutes. Our competitors charge $80–$100 just to show up — not including parts, labor, or consultation.

But we can't see charging you $80 to come to your home and push in an HDMI cord that was tugged a little too hard when the cleaning lady was dusting. Gas is $4 a gallon, The Villages and surrounding areas is a large place, and sometimes clients are 40 minutes from each other. We shouldn't be penalized for being efficient, and we're obviously working to support ourselves and our families.

So here's how we think about it: you're not paying for our time in your home today. You're paying for all the time it took us to learn what we're about to do to fix your issue with this kind of speed — while you were off learning other skills that enriched you.
