Pricing — Pay What You Feel It's Worth

For most services, we don't charge an hourly rate and we don't quote a price up front. After the work is done, you pay whatever amount reflects the value you feel we brought to your life.

We've worked this way for years and our clients have been generous. It works because we take the time to do the job right, and because there's no pressure and no awkward negotiation. If you feel we didn't help, you don't pay. Simple as that.

Exceptions: Three services have fixed prices because they include hardware or software we provide — the Golf Cart Theft Tracker ($100), Villages Gate RF Tag ($50–$100), and Microsoft Office 2021 Lifetime License ($75).

Why we don't charge by the hour

What you see as an insurmountable technical issue is often something we've fixed five times that week alone. We're in and out in most cases in minutes. Our competitors charge $80–$100 just to show up — not including parts, labor, or consultation.

But we can't see charging you $80 to come to your home and push in an HDMI cord that was tugged a little too hard when the cleaning lady was dusting. Gas is $4 a gallon, The Villages and surrounding areas is a large place, and sometimes clients are 40 minutes from each other. We shouldn't be penalized for being efficient, and we're obviously working to support ourselves and our families.

So here's how we think about it: you're not paying for our time in your home today. You're paying for all the time it took us to learn what we're about to do to fix your issue with this kind of speed — while you were off learning other skills that enriched you.
